# Canvas Issue Extraction

The canvas highlights delivery pressure around shifting client priorities, unclear ownership, and stretched coordination capacity.

## Issues

### Weekly priority churn is destabilizing delivery focus (`priority-churn`)

**Category:** delivery-risk  
**Priority:** high

Frequent priority changes can disrupt planning, reduce delivery predictability, and increase context switching across the team.

**Signals**

- Client priorities are changing weekly.
- Two mid-level engineers are split across support work and new feature delivery.

**Open questions**

- Which work items are most frequently being reshuffled?
- Is there an agreed weekly planning checkpoint with the client?

**Recommended next steps**

- Introduce a visible weekly reprioritization checkpoint.
- Separate protected delivery work from interrupt-driven support work.

### Unclear ownership is slowing escalations (`ownership-clarity`)

**Category:** coordination-risk  
**Priority:** high

If escalation paths are ambiguous, urgent issues take longer to resolve and stakeholder confidence can drop.

**Signals**

- Escalations are slow when ownership is unclear.
- One delivery lead is handling planning and stakeholder alignment.

**Open questions**

- Which decisions require explicit owners today?
- Are engineering and stakeholder escalation paths currently distinct?

**Recommended next steps**

- Define named owners for operational and delivery escalation paths.
- Publish a lightweight escalation matrix for the team and client.

### The team may be missing a stabilizing triage cadence (`triage-rhythm`)

**Category:** process-opportunity  
**Priority:** medium

A visible triage rhythm can absorb changing priorities more deliberately and reduce reactive churn.

**Signals**

- A visible triage rhythm could reduce churn.
- Senior engineering capacity is concentrated on a migration stream.

**Open questions**

- Who should own the triage cadence?
- How often should urgent support work be reviewed against migration commitments?

**Recommended next steps**

- Establish a recurring triage forum with clear intake rules.
- Review support interrupts against migration commitments each cycle.
